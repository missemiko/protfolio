About

A Little About Me

I'm MissEmiko. I enjoy building communities, supporting creators, managing Discord spaces, and helping creative projects grow.

Offline I'm Ayesha. Online, I'm usually somewhere in a Discord server, tidying up channels, welcoming new members or talking a creator through their next idea. Gaming and creative culture are a big part of my world, and they shape how I think about community.

I believe every successful community starts with good people, clear communication, and a welcoming environment. Everything else, the roles, the bots, the events, is just there to support that.

  • i.Good people come first, always.
  • ii.Clear communication keeps things calm.
  • iii.A welcoming space makes people stay.

02 What I Do

The things I spend my days on

Most of my work happens behind the scenes. When it's done well, a community just feels easy to be part of.

  1. Community Management

    Day-to-day care of a community: keeping conversations healthy, members heard and the mood friendly.

  2. Discord Server Management

    Roles, permissions, moderation and bots, set up so the server runs smoothly even when I'm offline.

  3. Creator Support

    Helping creators find their people, share their work and keep momentum when things get busy.

  4. Community Development

    Events, ideas and small rituals that help a group grow naturally, without forcing it.

  5. Server Setup & Organization

    Clean channel structures, clear rules and onboarding that makes sense from the very first click.

  6. Creative Projects

    Branding, server aesthetics and the little visual details that give a space its own personality.

04 FAQ

Questions people often ask

Can't find what you're looking for? Send me a message.

I'm Ayesha, known online as MissEmiko. I'm a community manager and creator supporter who builds and runs Discord communities, and I'm always around the gaming and creator world.

Right now there are three: Creator Nation for creators who want to connect and collaborate, Emiko Society for friendly conversation and new friends, and Minecraft Pakistan for Minecraft players and creators across Pakistan.

The easiest way is to join Creator Nation and reach out there, or send me a DM on Instagram. Tell me a little about what you make and what you have in mind.

Head to the My Communities section and press "Join Community" on any server. Each button opens its Discord invite in a new tab.

Instagram DMs are the quickest, and you can also find me in my Discord communities. All the options are in the contact section below.
